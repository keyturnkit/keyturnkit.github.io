# Thanks for your purchase

Bought on Gumroad? Your download is in the Gumroad receipt email.

Polar sends a confirmation email with an **Access purchase** button. That button opens your purchase on Polar, where the download is. If the email hasn't arrived, open [the Mam Studio customer portal](https://polar.sh/mam-studio/portal) and sign in with the email you paid with.

## Blog Import

Blog Import is free now, with no limit on posts and no license code to enter.

## Keyturn Kit Pro

Download the zip from your purchase (the Polar purchase page, or the Gumroad receipt email if you bought there), unzip it and follow `README.md`. `AGENTS.md` in the same folder tells Claude Code or Cursor how the license code is laid out.

## Something wrong?

Email [proj.seedsmesh@gmail.com](mailto:proj.seedsmesh@gmail.com) from the address you paid with, and we'll sort it out. Keyturn Kit Pro has a full refund within 14 days if it doesn't work for you.
