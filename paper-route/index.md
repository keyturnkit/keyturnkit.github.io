# Paper Route: a free text-only newsletter template for Framer

Paper Route is a free template we made for a letter that is mostly words. The home page lists every issue, newest first, with its title and date, and each issue gets a plain reading page inside a cobalt frame. There are no cover images to find.

## Bring your archive in

The 15 issues in the template are samples. The [Blog Import](/blog-import/) plugin replaces them with yours: open the CMS, select the Issues collection and click Manage, choose your Substack, Ghost, WordPress, Medium, Blogger or Squarespace export, pick "1 collection, everything inside each post" and tick "Remove the 15 posts already in this collection". Publish, and the list and every issue page fill from your posts.

Your newest 25 posts import free. A Blog Import license imports the rest: $29 for one Framer project or $59 for any number, paid once.

## What's included

- Archive list with every issue, newest first
- Issue page with title, date, a one-line summary, the full text and a link to the next issue
- About page, and a subscribe form on the archive and issue pages
- Light and dark colour schemes that follow the reader's system setting
- 404 page and a Read me page with the setup steps
- Google Fonts only (Fraunces, Archivo)

## Limits

The template shows text only, so posts with photos keep them in the body but get no cover. Redirects from your old addresses need Framer Pro or higher; on Basic, Blog Import copies the old and new addresses as a CSV. Set where sign-ups go in the subscribe form's Send to setting.

Want covers? [Slack Tide](/slack-tide/) is our free archive with a photo grid, and [Back Issue](/back-issue/) our magazine template.
