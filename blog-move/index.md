# Want help moving your blog to Framer?

Most people move their blog themselves with [Blog Import](/blog-import/), which is free with no limit on posts. If you'd rather have help, email us and we'll reply.

<p><a class="btn btn-primary" href="mailto:proj.seedsmesh@gmail.com?subject=Help%20moving%20my%20blog%20to%20Framer">Email us</a></p>

Tell us where the blog is now (Squarespace, WordPress, Blogger, Medium or Ghost), about how many posts it has, and which Framer plan you're on. Please don't attach your export file to the first email.

The guides cover each step if you want to do it yourself: [Squarespace](/guides/squarespace-blog-to-framer/), [WordPress](/guides/wordpress-blog-to-framer/), [Blogger](/guides/blogger-blog-to-framer/), [Medium](/guides/medium-blog-to-framer/) and [Ghost](/guides/ghost-blog-to-framer/).
