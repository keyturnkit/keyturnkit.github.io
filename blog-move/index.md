# We move your blog to Framer

If you'd rather not run the import yourself, we'll do it with [Blog Import](/blog-import/), the plugin we make, and check the result by hand. $99 per blog, paid once.

## What you get

- Every post from your Squarespace, WordPress, Blogger, Medium or Ghost blog in your Framer CMS, with title, date, author, categories, tags, cover and body.
- The images inside posts copied into Framer, so they keep working after you cancel the old site.
- A redirect from every old post address to the new one, if your Framer plan includes redirects. On Framer Basic, a file of the redirects to add when you upgrade.
- A short note listing anything we couldn't bring over, such as embeds we don't support.
- Done within 2 working days of getting your export and the invite.

## What it doesn't include

Designing the blog pages, moving pages that aren't blog posts, comments, forms or shop products. If your Framer project has no blog pages yet, we connect the posts to a plain blog layout you can restyle, or to your template's.

## How it works

1. Pay below. You get a confirmation email.
2. Reply to it with your blog's export file (or its address, for a live WordPress site) and invite proj.seedsmesh@gmail.com to your Framer project as an editor.
3. We import, check a sample of posts on desktop and phone, and email you when it's done. You remove our access afterwards.

If we can't move your blog, for example the export won't open, you get a full refund.
